# **App Name**: AgriPulse AI

## Core Features:

- AI-Powered Crop & Weather Advisory: Utilizes an AI tool to interpret real-time weather and crop growth stage data, generating hyper-localized, actionable advice on disease risk, optimal irrigation, and effective spray windows for specific fields.
- Field Health Monitoring Dashboard: Provides farmers with a visual dashboard of field health, integrating satellite imagery (NDVI) to identify areas of stress, nutrient deficiency, or pest outbreaks within their fields.
- Real-time Market Price & Input Cost Tracking: Aggregates commodity futures prices, local market data, and currency exchange rates to inform optimal selling times for produce and purchasing decisions for agricultural inputs.
- Carbon Footprint & Sustainability Reporting: Automatically calculates and reports the carbon footprint of various farm operations, integrating with carbon calculation APIs to assist in potential carbon credit applications.
- Smart Irrigation Scheduling: Calculates daily evapotranspiration (ET) rates for each field using weather data to provide precise, data-driven recommendations for irrigation volumes, optimizing water usage.
- Multi-Channel Notifications: Delivers critical weather advisories, market alerts, and irrigation recommendations directly to farmers via SMS or email, ensuring accessibility even in low-connectivity areas.
- Farm & Field Profile Management: Allows farmers to securely define and manage their farm's properties, including field boundaries, crop types, planting dates, and soil characteristics, for personalized insights.

## Style Guidelines:

- The color scheme is light, reflecting natural environments and clear data visualization.
- Primary color: A deep, earthy forest green (#478F23), signifying growth, sustainability, and data intelligence without being overly literal.
- Background color: A subtly tinted off-white (#F1F4F0) that provides a clean, breathable canvas, allowing the data and visualizations to stand out.
- Accent color: A vibrant, fresh lime green (#63EB47), used for calls to action, highlights, and critical alert indicators to provide clear contrast and immediate attention.
- Headline font: 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif), chosen for its modern, tech-forward, and slightly scientific aesthetic, reflecting the advanced data capabilities of the platform.
- Body text font: 'Inter' (sans-serif), providing a clean, highly legible, and objective feel for displaying detailed data and advisory content.
- Code snippets or developer-facing elements (if any): 'Source Code Pro' (monospace), for clarity and ease of reading technical information.
- Utilize a set of modern, clear line-art icons that subtly represent agricultural themes (e.g., leaf, cloud, tractor) and data visualization concepts (e.g., charts, maps).
- Dashboard-oriented layout with an emphasis on intuitive data visualization. Employ clean grids and modular components to present complex information clearly, ensuring responsiveness across devices, particularly mobile.
- Incorporate subtle, informative animations for data loading, chart transitions, and notification pop-ups to provide clear user feedback and enhance interactivity without distraction.
